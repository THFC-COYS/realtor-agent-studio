Set the approval matrix, then name the owner

This is the governance step, and for a Realtor it is the whole product. Your agent will talk to leads and clients in your name, so the defaults are conservative: it drafts freely, and anything that sends, posts publicly, or schedules waits for your word.

Studio rule: no owner, no agent. A Realtor's agent without a named accountable owner does not ship. The final package will not generate until the owner is named.
